# ADR 0003: XGBoost and explicit deferral

- Status: Provisional
- Context: The brief names XGBoost and reliability-aware abstention.
- Decision: Include XGBoost alongside Logistic Regression and Random Forest. Current P-tier probability bins are provisional software behavior only.
- Consequences: No clinical or calibrated reliability claim is permitted until thresholds are evaluated on held-out data and justified by an explicit decision analysis.
